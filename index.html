About

This past summer, I worked as an Investment Banking Summer Analyst at Tobin & Company Investment Banking Group, where I supported private capital raising transactions targeting $200M+ in aggregate proceeds — researching PPM terms, drafting CIM content, and building DCF and comparable company valuation models across live deals.

Outside of that, I’ve built a 3-statement operating model as a Modeling Extern for the Investing and Financial Modeling Program, run market research for a boutique asset manager, and written independent equity research on public companies. At Rutgers, I’m involved with Little Investment Bankers of Rutgers (LIBOR), the Corporate Finance Society, and the Personal Finance Club, where I lead workshops on budgeting and investing.

Outside of finance: snowboarding, golf, weightlifting, MMA, and cheering (through it all) for the Giants and Knicks.
